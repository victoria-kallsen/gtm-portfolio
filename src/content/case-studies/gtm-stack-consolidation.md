## My process

1. **Audit.** I listed every tool with its seats, cost, owner and real usage.
2. **Map overlap.** I charted which jobs each tool does, and where two tools were doing the same one.
3. **Consolidate.** I chose the tools to keep and rebuilt the missing workflows inside them.
4. **Migrate.** I moved the data and automations across, then shut down the old tools with no downtime.
